Domynice Higher & Lower Bot

Domynice Higher & Lower Bot is a Deriv trading bot designed for Higher and Lower contracts on Continuous and Synthetic/Derived Indices.

Features

- Deriv account connection
- Continuous and Synthetic/Derived Indices
- Higher and Lower contracts
- Live market prices
- Configurable stake
- Contract duration
- Barrier configuration
- Take Profit
- Stop Loss
- Maximum consecutive losses
- Maximum trades
- Demo and Real account support
- Trade history and results
- Start and Stop controls
